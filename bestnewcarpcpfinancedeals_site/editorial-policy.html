Editorial & Review Policy

At BestNewCarPCPFinanceDeals.com, we hold our content to strict editorial standards to ensure our audience receives accurate, unbiased, and practical information regarding car finance options in the UK.

1. Editorial Independence

Our reviews and comparisons are written independently by our automotive finance team. Financial providers do not pay us to alter review scores or manipulate evaluations. While we may receive referral commissions through affiliate links on our portal, this does not influence our objective evaluation criteria.

2. Accuracy and Data Currency

Representative APR rates, lender requirements, and UK consumer regulations change frequently. We review and update our comparison tables and guides regularly (current as of July 2026) to reflect accurate market conditions.

3. FCA & Compliance Awareness

We strongly emphasize consumer rights regulated by the UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). We always encourage visitors to read individual lender agreement terms, verify representative APR eligibility, and understand Consumer Credit Act rights prior to signing finance agreements.
