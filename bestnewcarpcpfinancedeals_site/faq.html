Car PCP Finance FAQs

What is Personal Contract Purchase (PCP)?

Personal Contract Purchase is a flexible car finance agreement where you pay fixed monthly installments based on the car’s predicted depreciation over a 24 to 48 month period, rather than paying off the full market value.

Can I get a PCP deal with no deposit (£0 Deposit)?

Yes, many UK finance brokers offer £0 deposit options. However, putting down a deposit reduces your monthly payments and overall interest accrued.

What is a Balloon Payment (GMFV)?

The Guaranteed Minimum Future Value (GMFV) is the estimated residual value of the car at the end of the contract set by the lender. If you wish to keep the car at the end of the agreement, you pay this optional balloon payment.

Will applying for a quote hurt my credit score?

Most reputable UK car finance comparison platforms utilize a 'soft credit check' for initial eligibility tools, which is invisible to other lenders and does not impact your credit score.

What happens if I exceed my agreed mileage limit?

If you decide to return the car at the end of the contract and have exceeded the annual mileage allowance, you will be charged an excess mileage fee (typically a few pence per mile) specified in your original agreement.
