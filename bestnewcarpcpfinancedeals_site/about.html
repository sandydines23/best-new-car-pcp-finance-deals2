About Best New Car PCP Finance Deals

Welcome to BestNewCarPCPFinanceDeals.com, an independent UK buyer guide dedicated to helping consumers navigate the complex world of Personal Contract Purchase (PCP) finance.

Our Mission

Financing a new car is one of the largest financial decisions many households make. With dozens of credit brokers, lenders, and varying APR rates available across the UK market, finding clear, unbiased information can be challenging. Our mission is to simplify this process by reviewing top-rated PCP brokers, breaking down technical terms, and helping you find transparent finance deals.

How We Evaluate Providers

Our editorial team systematically reviews brokers based on criteria that matter most to UK car buyers:

  • Representative APR Rates: Transparency of interest rates and fee structures.
  • Application Process: Ease of eligibility checks and availability of soft credit searches.
  • Lender Panel Depth: The diversity of financial institution partnerships to accommodate various credit profiles.
  • Customer Feedback: Verified user satisfaction ratings across independent review networks.

Independent Information Portal

We do not issue credit directly or act as a licensed mortgage or credit broker. Our goal is strictly informational to help you make well-informed decisions before applying.
