What is PCP Car Finance & How Does It Work?

Personal Contract Purchase (PCP) is currently one of the most popular ways to finance a new or nearly-new car in the UK. Unlike traditional Hire Purchase (HP) or personal loans, PCP offers significantly lower monthly payments because you are essentially paying off the vehicle’s predicted depreciation during the agreement term rather than its full value.

Key Features of a New Car PCP Deal

  • Initial Deposit: Usually between 0% and 20% of the vehicle’s purchase price. Higher deposits lead to lower monthly commitments.
  • Fixed Monthly Payments: Calculated based on the expected value loss over 24, 36, or 48 months.
  • Guaranteed Minimum Future Value (GMFV): The estimated value of the car at the end of the contract, set by the lender at the start.
  • End-of-Term Options: You have 3 flexible choices when the contract ends:
    1. Pay the GMFV (Balloon Payment) to own the car outright.
    2. Hand the keys back to the finance provider with nothing further to pay (subject to mileage and condition limits).
    3. Part-exchange the vehicle for a new model using any equity above the GMFV.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a PCP finance deal with no deposit?

Yes, many UK lenders on platforms like Car-Finance.co.uk offer zero-deposit PCP agreements, subject to status and credit verification.

Does applying for a PCP quote affect my credit score?

Initial eligibility checks use a 'soft credit search' that does not impact your credit rating or show up to other lenders.

Can I end my PCP deal early?

Yes, under UK law (Consumer Credit Act), you have the right to request an early settlement quote from your finance provider at any point during your agreement.
